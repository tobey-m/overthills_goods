Brand Story & Concept

The Name & Origin

Derived from "Over the Hill" by Ginger Root, combined with the vintage, artistic vibes of Zhongshan second-hand shops in Taiwan.

Concept

We are building a "Street Running Lifestyle" brand. It's not just a technical sports shirt; it's an aesthetic piece you can wear for a long run, and seamlessly wear to a cafe afterwards.

The Philosophy: "Run Ran Run"

RUN (Present / Future)

People who are actively running, or getting inspired to start. Welcoming newcomers to the community.

RAN (Past / Experience)

Giving respect to those who have logged the miles, or even those resting from injuries.

RUN (The Cycle)

The never-ending cycle of the sport. An endless loop of returning to the pavement or trail.
