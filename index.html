CHIANG MAISTREET RUNNING LIFESTYLE

The name comes from “Over the Hill” by Ginger Root. The mood draws from the vintage, artistic atmosphere of Zhongshan’s second-hand shops in Taiwan.

Rooted in Chiang Mai. Built around a street running lifestyle: a long run, a cafe afterwards, and the rest of the day.

Road and Trail are two sides of the same idea.
